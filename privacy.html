Privacy Policy

Kids Reels · Last updated: 10 October 2026

Who we are

Kids Reels is a small project that publishes short educational videos for children on its own YouTube channels. It includes an automated tool (the "kids-reels" application) that uploads those videos.

What the application accesses

The application uses Google's YouTube Data API only to upload videos to, and read basic information about, YouTube channels owned by the Kids Reels operator. It is not offered to the public, and no one else signs in to it.

  • youtube.upload: to upload our own finished videos.
  • youtube.readonly: to confirm which of our channels a video is being uploaded to.

Data we collect

We do not collect, store, sell or share personal information from viewers, children or anyone else. The application does not read comments, viewer data or analytics. The only credential stored is the operator's own YouTube authorization token, kept encrypted in a private settings store, and used solely to upload our videos.

Children

Our videos are made for children and are marked "Made for Kids" on YouTube, as required by the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We do not knowingly collect any information from children. Viewing our videos on YouTube is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Kids Reels' use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. You can revoke the application's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
